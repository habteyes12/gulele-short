The Drunkard of Gulele

Tebeje, Gulele's famous chicken trader and drunkard, builds a house of his own and swears off drink for its honour. Again and again. Narrated in Amharic, with English subtitles, and made with AI from one storyboard file.

The film

A 88-second cut for phones and feeds, and a 4-minute family cut that opens with the story's first four passages, word for word.

The story

In 1948 (1941 E.C.) Temesgen Gebre published Yegulelew Sekaram, which Asfaw Damte and Zerihun Asfaw credit as the first Amharic short story. It is funny and merciless. Gulele laughs at Tebeje even when he dives into a flood to save a drowning maid and pulls out a pig. After nearly two sober years he dreams the price of one more drink, and wakes to ask his housekeeper how many legs he has.

በጉለሌ የነበረው የታወቀ ዶሮ ነጋዴ ማነው ብለው ከገፈርሳ እስከ ደጃዝማች ይገዙ ሠፈር ለጠየቁ ሰዎች፤ ተበጀ፣ ሰካራሙ ተበጀ፣ ዶሮ ነጋዴው ተበጀ ነው ብለው ይነግሯቸዋል።

ወደ ሰማይ እያየ ‹መጠጥ ማለት ለእኔ ሞት ማለት ነው› አለ። ጊዜውም ሌሊት ነበር። ‹እንደዚህ የማሉበት ሌሊት ያልፋል› ብለው ወይዘሮ ጥሩነሽ መለሱለት።

Looking at the sky he said, 'For me, drink means death.' It was night. 'Nights of such vows pass,' Weyzero Tiruneshe replied.

Laughing with everyone

Gossip at the coffee fire

This was true. Whether worse than they said or better, he came out of Gulele's eucalyptus and appeared to Addis Ababa as a strange new man.

By the old timber customs, heading east, across the bridge over the river, the first small thatched hut you reach: that is Tebeje's house. There, in that house, in Gulele, he lived.

How it was made

One JSON file, storyboard.json, is the contract between five stages. Every stage caches by a hash of its inputs, so changing one line regenerates one shot. A clean clone rebuilds the film with zero model calls.

Storyboard

Shots, prompts, narration, subtitles and cut points, validated by a JSON Schema. storyboard.json

Narration

Licensed audiobook excerpts located by transcription and snapped to the narrator's pauses. align.py

Keyframes

FLUX.1 schnell on Cloudflare Workers AI, with a cinematic style block and a fixed character. keyframes.py

Motion

Eased Ken Burns moves per shot and aspect ratio. Wan 2.2 clips drop in when available. shots.py

Assembly

Cut to the voice, ducked score, two-pass loudness, burned-in subtitles, AI end card. assemble.py

What broke, and what fixed it

Free tiers, quotas and models that ignore instructions. Each is logged in FAILURES.md.

No FFmpeg on the machine

added `imageio-ffmpeg` to requirements. It bundles a static FFmpeg 9 build with libass, zoompan, sidechaincompress and loudnorm (checked with `ffmpeg -filters`). `pipeline/common.py:ffmpeg_exe` uses the system FFmpeg if one is on PATH, otherwise the bundled on

No `make` on Windows

`python film.py` is the real entry point. The `Makefile` is a thin wrapper for machines that have `make`.

No `ffprobe` either

WAV durations come from Python's `wave` module. Other durations are parsed from `ffmpeg -i` stderr (`common.media_duration`).

Cloudflare FLUX.1 schnell rejects `seed`

set `models.image.send_seed: false` in `storyboard.json`. The seed is still logged and stays in the cache hash.

Narration 3× too long: 268 s film

shortened the instruction and changed the prompt to the documented `"Say ...: <text>"` form (`narration.py`). Status: TODO after quota resets / on another model.

Gemini TTS free tier: 10 requests per day per model

`raise_for_http` now fails fast when a 429 says "per day". Per-minute 429s still retry. Because the quota is per model, the remaining runs today use a different TTS model; it is used for all 13 shots so the voice stays the same.

The TTS voice did not sound like the story

the narrator gave permission (ETHICS.md), so the film now uses excerpts of his real reading. `narration.py` got an `audio_source` path: it cuts `[start, end]` segments from a licensed recording instead of calling TTS. It is cached and logged as `external:<file

The transcription model ignores timestamp instructions

`pipeline/align.py` locates each target sentence by fuzzy match in the chunk text, converts its character position into a time estimate, and snaps the estimate to the narrator's real pauses.
